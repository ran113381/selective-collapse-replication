# Coders' follow-up answer to Question 5 (the 2-vs-3 boundary, in their own words)

This file excerpts only the fifth item of a written, self-administered
follow-up questionnaire sent to the two coders after the 70-question
adjudication meeting, and their answers to it. No other item of that
questionnaire, and no personal, institutional or contact information, is
reproduced here. The coders are identified only as **coder A** and **coder
B**, matching the labels used throughout this package (`coding_sheet_A_v2.csv`,
`coding_sheet_B_v2.csv`); coder A is the coder who filled the "A" slot in the
revised questionnaire, coder B the coder who filled the "B" slot.

## Question 5 (original Chinese)

> 5. 经过这轮讨论，如果请你在评分标准里补写一句"2 分和 3 分到底怎么分"的
> 说明，你会怎么写？请用你自己的话。

**English translation, not given to any rater:**

> 5. After this round of discussion, if you were asked to add a sentence to
> the rubric explaining exactly how to draw the line between a 2 and a 3,
> how would you write it? Please answer in your own words.

## Coder A's answer (filled 2026-08-23)

Original Chinese:

> 3分：有标准模板套路，内容很全面，但是需要把一部分变量换成自己的。
> 2分：缺少必要的代码内容，没办法解决问题

**English translation, not given to any rater:**

> A 3: there is a standard template pattern, the content is comprehensive, but
> some variables need to be swapped for the asker's own.
> A 2: it lacks necessary code content and cannot be solved.

## Coder B's answer (filled 2026-08-24)

Original Chinese:

> 2分是题目条件不明确，不能解决问题，还缺少必要条件，3分是条件足够明确

*(second answer box, verbatim)*

> 不需要再补充，但是还需要结合代码解决具体问题。

**English translation, not given to any rater:**

> A 2 is when the question's conditions are unclear, it cannot be solved, and
> necessary conditions are still missing; a 3 is when the conditions are
> sufficiently clear.

*(second answer box)*

> Nothing more needs to be added, but it still needs to be
> combined with code to solve the specific problem.

## Provenance

Both answers were extracted from the `word/document.xml` text-box content
(`w:txbxContent`) of the coders' completed follow-up forms, which are not
themselves included in this package (the package holds only the blank forms,
`P9_裁决补充问题包_20260824/02_过程与背景_编码员A.docx` and
`03_过程与背景_编码员B.docx`). Section 4.4 of the manuscript reports these two
answers, condensed, as converging on *information sufficiency*.
